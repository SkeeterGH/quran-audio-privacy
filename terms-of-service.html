Using Quran Audio

You may use Quran Audio to listen to recitations, save ayahs and organize your listening. Use the app in accordance with applicable law and the terms of the store through which you obtained it.

Offline listening and downloads

The included Mishary Rashid Alafasy recitation is available for offline playback after installation. Optional recitations are downloaded from EveryAyah.com and require an internet connection and sufficient storage. Once a recitation is downloaded, you can listen to it offline. Your internet provider may charge for data, and optional downloads depend on the availability of the audio provider.
