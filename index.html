As Smart Systems provides Quran Audio, an offline Quran listening app. Contact us at assmartsystemsgh@gmail.com.

Data kept on your device

Your bookmarks, personal notes, favorite surahs, listening position, queue, downloaded audio and preferences are stored on your device. The app does not send these items to As Smart Systems. There is no account, cloud sync, advertising SDK or analytics service in the app.

Audio and internet connections

The complete included recitation is delivered with the Android installation and can be played offline. If you choose to download another reciter, the app connects to EveryAyah.com over HTTPS. That service necessarily receives connection information, such as your IP address and the requested audio files, and may retain server logs under its own practices. We do not send your bookmarks, notes or listening history with these requests. Installed recitations play locally without contacting an audio server.

Device access

The app checks connection status to respect your Wi-Fi-only choice and checks available storage before downloading audio. Background media playback uses an Android foreground media service and media controls. Audio files are kept in app-private storage. The app does not request access to your contacts, precise location, camera or microphone.

Your choices and deletion

You can remove bookmarks and notes in Saved and remove optional recitations in Audio storage. Included recordings form part of the installation. Clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling it removes locally stored app data. The Android release excludes app data from Android cloud backup and device transfer; some manufacturer-controlled system behavior is outside the app’s control.

Support correspondence

If you email us, your email provider sends us your email address and anything you include. We use this information to answer your request and resolve support issues. Do not include private notes or sensitive information unless necessary. You may ask us to delete support correspondence, subject to any retention obligations.

Children and changes

The app has no social messaging, user profiles, or targeted advertising. We do not knowingly collect children’s personal information through the app. The app can be used without an account or providing personal details. If practices change, we will update this policy and its effective date.
